1

General Rules

All evaluation collectors must follow these rules:

  • Students must evaluate their teachers anonymously and independently.
  • Teachers must not be present while their students complete the evaluation.
  • Collectors must remain neutral and must not influence students' answers.
  • Students must not write their names or any identifying information on their answer sheets.
  • Each group must be evaluated separately, even if several groups have the same teacher.
  • Answer sheets from different groups must never be mixed.
  • All collected papers must be kept secure and confidential.
  • Students must not discuss or compare their answers during the evaluation.
  • Teachers must not collect, inspect, or handle completed answer sheets.
  • Every distributed answer sheet must be accounted for.
2

Before Entering a Classroom

The collector must:

  • Check the evaluation schedule and identify the assigned classroom.
  • Confirm the teacher's full name.
  • Confirm the group's lesson days, lesson time, and level.
  • Prepare enough questionnaires for all students present.
  • Prepare a separate envelope or folder for that group.
  • Ensure that pens are available if needed.
Important: Prepare a separate envelope for every group, including groups taught by the same teacher.
3

Entering the Classroom

  1. Knock and enter the classroom politely.
  2. Inform the teacher that the classroom is scheduled for an anonymous teacher evaluation.
  3. Politely ask the teacher to leave the classroom temporarily.
  4. Wait until the teacher has completely left before explaining the evaluation to students.
  5. Make sure the teacher cannot observe the students' answers.
  6. Ask students to sit quietly and listen to the instructions.
If the teacher refuses to leave: Do not argue. Contact the evaluation coordinator and record the incident. Do not conduct the evaluation with the teacher present.
4

Instructions to Read to Students

Collectors should explain the process clearly, using the following script:

Hello, everyone!

Today, you will be completing a short questionnaire to evaluate your teacher. This is only to improve our education system.

There are 10 multiple-choice questions. Please read each question carefully and choose the answer that best reflects your personal opinion.

This evaluation is completely anonymous.

  • Do not write your name anywhere on the paper.
  • Answer honestly based on your own experience with the teacher.
  • There are no right or wrong answers.
  • Your answers will not affect your grades, results, or relationship with your teacher.
  • Do not discuss your answers with other students.
  • Your teacher will not see your individual answer sheet.

Your honest feedback will help us understand what is working well and what needs improvement.

Please take your time and answer every question carefully.

Thank you for your cooperation!

The script is subject to adjustments according to group level.

5

How Students Must Mark Their Answers

Before distributing the questionnaires, collectors must explain how to complete them.

  • Each question has several possible answers.
  • Students must select only ONE answer per question.
  • Students must clearly circle or tick their selected option, following the marking example provided.
  • If students make a mistake, they should clearly cross out the incorrect selection and mark their final answer.
  • Students must answer all 10 questions if possible.
  • Students must not write their names, signatures, or other identifying details.
  • Students must work independently without consulting classmates.

Example — try it

Question 1: How clearly does your teacher explain lessons?

If a student chooses B, they should clearly circle the letter B.

Before students begin, the collector must ask:

“Does everyone understand how to mark their answers?”

If necessary, demonstrate the marking process using a separate sample sheet or the board.

Important: Collectors may explain how to mark answers but must not suggest which answers students should choose.
6

During the Evaluation

Collectors must

  • Stay inside the classroom throughout the evaluation.
  • Ensure that students work individually.
  • Prevent discussions or answer sharing.
  • Avoid standing over students or looking at their selected answers.
  • Answer procedural questions only.
  • Maintain a quiet, comfortable environment.
  • Give students sufficient time to finish.

Strictly prohibited

  • Suggesting that students give positive or negative ratings.
  • Telling students how they should evaluate their teacher.
  • Discussing personal opinions about the teacher.
  • Photographing completed answer sheets.
  • Allowing teachers to enter and observe the evaluation.
  • Asking students to identify their papers after submission.
7

Collecting Completed Answer Sheets

Once students finish:

  • Ask students to stop writing.
  • Collect all answer sheets directly from students.
  • Place the sheets immediately into the designated group envelope.
  • Count the collected sheets without inspecting individual answers.
  • Verify that the number of collected sheets matches the number distributed.
  • Record any missing, unused, or spoiled sheets.
  • Seal the envelope before leaving the classroom.
Do not sort papers by students' answers or attempt to identify individual students.
8

Group Identification and Labeling

Every group's completed answer sheets must be stored in a separate envelope. Each envelope must have the following label:

Teacher NameLesson DayLesson TimeGroup LevelNumber of Students

Same teacher, two groups — these must be stored in two separate envelopes.

Required envelope information

  • Teacher's full name
  • Regular lesson days
  • Regular lesson starting time
  • Group level
  • Number of students who submitted an evaluation
  • Actual evaluation date
  • Collector's name (on the envelope only)
Important: The number of students on the label must represent the number of submitted questionnaires, not the total number enrolled in the group.
Never write students' names on the envelope or answer sheets.
9

Handling Multiple Groups and the Two-Day Schedule

Since teachers may teach several groups, evaluation collection can continue for up to two days. Collectors must follow these procedures:

  • Maintain a checklist of all groups scheduled for evaluation.
  • Ensure that no group is evaluated twice accidentally.
  • Preserve each group's original envelope without combining it with another group's papers.
  • At the end of Day 1, submit collected envelopes to the evaluation coordinator for secure storage.
  • At the beginning of Day 2, review the list of pending groups.
  • Once collection is finished, verify that every scheduled group has been accounted for.
Important: If a teacher teaches five groups, there must be five separately labeled envelopes.
10

End-of-Day Handover

After completing their assigned classrooms, collectors must:

  • Count the total number of completed group envelopes.
  • Verify that every envelope has a complete identification label.
  • Confirm that each envelope contains papers from only one group.
  • Check that the collected questionnaire counts are recorded.
  • Update the evaluation tracking sheet.
  • Report any missed groups or unusual incidents.
  • Hand all sealed envelopes to the designated evaluation coordinator.
Collectors must not take completed questionnaires home or leave them unattended.
